Project Overview

Development of an Integrated Enterprise Resource Planning System for Small Businesses.

About the project

Academic project

This project explores how a single, easy-to-use platform can help small businesses organize everyday operations. Many small businesses depend on notebooks, spreadsheets and separate applications for accounts, stock, customers and employees. This can lead to repeated data entry, mistakes and difficulty getting a clear view of the business. The proposed ERP brings key activities together in one place.

◎

Problem addressed

Scattered records, manual processes, repeated work and limited business visibility.

✦

Project objective

Provide a centralized and approachable business management solution for small organizations.

⌘

Expected benefit

Better organization, fewer manual steps and easier access to business information.

Planned technology architecture

Frontend

React.js, HTML, CSS and JavaScript

Backend

Node.js and Express.js

Database

PostgreSQL

Technology stack is based on the earlier project-report drafts; update this section if your implemented stack differs.

Limitations & future scope

Current limitations: This prototype uses sample data and does not connect to a production database, payment gateway, GST portal or bank account. Full access control, backups and deployment hardening would be required before real business use.

Future enhancements: GST and tax tools, secure online payments, mobile applications, automated payroll, cloud deployment, stronger role-based security, analytics and AI-assisted inventory forecasting.
